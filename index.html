Reach the rest of the internet.

OpenLink is a small VPN that helps you visit websites and apps that don't load where you are. Turn it on when you need it. Turn it off when you don't.

Sign up as a tester

What it does

Some sites and apps are blocked in some places. OpenLink routes your connection through a server somewhere else, so those sites work again.

How it works

  1. You download and install our app.
  2. Scan the QR code that we send you.
  3. Connect.

That's it. No account, no login, no email forms.

What it costs

Free while testing. Subscription fees shows up later, we'll tell you first.

What it can't do

  • It's not magic — sometimes it's slower, very rarely it's down.
  • It doesn't make you anonymous. Sites you log into still know it's you.
  • It's fine for browsing, messaging, work. Not optimized for 4K streaming.
